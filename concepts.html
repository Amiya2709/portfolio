01 · Eyewear & optics · Magrabi Optical

Two Climates

In the UAE your eyes go from 45°C sun to 19°C air-conditioning many times a day. Most people never connect that to dry, tired eyes.

Four everyday UAE lives (office worker, student, school-run parent, delivery rider) each show their two-climate day, then an optometrist explains why it matters. Filmed in Arabic and English.

46°COUTSIDE
“My eyes live in two countries every day.”
Hook 1A phone weather screen at 46°C, then a pan to the office thermostat at 19°C.
Hook 2“I thought it was screen time. It was the AC.”
Hook 3Optometrist to camera: “If you live in the UAE, here’s what your eyes are dealing with.”

02 · Food delivery · talabat

Six Nationalities, One Order

In UAE flatshares, choosing dinner means negotiating across six cuisines, and the group chat is where the drama happens.

Real flatmates film the chaos as screen recordings of the group chat cut with reactions, ending with everyone getting their own dish in one order. Fixed ending, swappable hooks, built for Reels and TikTok.

03 · Rides · Careem · Ramadan

The Last 11 Minutes

During Ramadan, the minutes before iftar are the most time-sensitive of the day. Getting home to family in time matters.

A real-time countdown series: creators film their journey to iftar with a running clock on screen, ending at the family table. Warm and respectful, built around the moment rather than the ride, with a cultural review before filming.

11:00UNTIL IFTAR
“Iftar in 11 minutes. I’m still in Business Bay.”
Hook 1A clock on screen: “Iftar in 11 minutes. I’m still in Business Bay.”
Hook 2“My mother doesn’t wait. Neither does iftar.”
Hook 3A silent shot of an empty chair at the table, timer running.
