“Single-creator ads wear out fast. So we treated footage like building blocks.”

01

The brief

A set number of fresh ads every month from a limited pool of creator footage.

02

The idea

Mashups: the strongest lines from several real customers in one ad. Keep the body and call to action fixed, swap the hook, and every set becomes a clean test.

03

The moves

Themed sets of three, a hook for each. Spanish versions with their own editing guide. Brand rules turned into editing guidelines so every editor cut the same way.

04

What changed

142 ads across 31 ad sets from January to November, tracked in one master log.
