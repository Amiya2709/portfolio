If a line sounds like a brand wrote it, rewrite it.

02 · Hooks

The first three seconds decide everything else.

Rule 1Say it and show it

The first line works with the sound off. On-screen text repeats the hook.

Rule 2Faces beat logos

Open on a person, never a product shot.

Rule 3One idea per hook

Needs a second sentence to make sense? Too complicated.

Rule 4Always write three

The hook is the cheapest part to change and moves results most.

06 · Strategy beyond the script

Build in sets. Test the hook. Refresh before it fades.

An ad set = one body, one CTA, three hooks
1AConfession hookSame bodySame CTA
1BPOV hookSame bodySame CTA
1CExpert hookSame bodySame CTA

Only the hook changes, so the results tell you which opening works.

Casting
Cast the situation, not the demographic.

Not “woman, 30s”. “A nurse on night shifts who’s never had time to compare options.”

Objections
Say the doubt out loud first.

“I thought buying a car online would be risky, until I saw there’s a return window.”

Mashups
Best lines from several creators, one ad.

One shoot powers many ads, and every voice is extra proof.

Where it runs changes how it’s cut
Feeds

9:16, fast cuts, captions on, native and unpolished.

Sound off

Every ad works silent, with burned-in captions.

PDP
Product pages

Calmer, explanatory, answers buyer questions.

ENESAR
Languages

Re-cast or re-edit per language. Not just subtitles.

07 · Regulated categoriespharma & health

Make approved claims sound human.

Claims as approved

Creators rephrase around a claim. The claim itself never changes.

Experience, not promises

“It helped my eyes feel less dry” ✓   “It cures dry eye” ✗

Review before filming

Medical, legal and regulatory see the script first. Fixing a script beats a reshoot.

Traceable

Every approved asset gets a reference code. Every script change is logged.

Consent first

Everyone on screen signs before the ad runs.

08 · Metrics that matter

Read them together, not one at a time.

Reflects: the hookHook rate

Share who watch past 3 seconds.

Reflects: problem, solution, pacingHold rate

How long people keep watching.

Reflects: relatabilityEngagement

Likes, comments, shares, saves.

Reflects: the CTA & offerClick-through

Share who click the link.

Reflects: offer + landing pageConversion

Share who buy or sign up.

Reflects: everythingCost per acquisition

What each customer costs.

Diagnose it

The opening works. The rest of the ad doesn’t convince. Fix the body, the proof or the offer.

The rest of the ad never gets a chance. Test new hooks before touching anything else.

Meters are illustrative, not real campaign data.
