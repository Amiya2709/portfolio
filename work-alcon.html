“Most compliant ads sound like a label read out loud. Ours sounded like friends talking about their eyes.”

01

The brief

A monthly flow of UGC ads for four eye-care lines. Every claim cleared by medical, legal and regulatory. Every creator with signed consent.

02

The idea

Two voices in one ad. Everyday users talk about the moment of relief; eye doctors give the expert reason why.

03

The moves

Rewrote each creator's own story into a compliant script. Made a standalone verbal disclosure a default ask. Never let the product sit beside a "problem" line.

04

What changed

Legal feedback became standing rules, so the same rejection never had to be learned twice. Status updates went from hours of copying to seconds.
