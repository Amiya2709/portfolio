How the sausage gets made

The playbook.

Ten stages every campaign moved through, and eleven lessons I paid for the hard way.

10 stagesscroll sideways →
01

Plan

Monthly targets per client. Tracker, calendar and info page set up before anything else.

02

Cast

Personas and life situations, not just age and gender. A vetted roster of proven creators.

03

Brief

Objective, must-say claims, don’t-say list, filming specs, example scripts.

04

Script

Mashup sets: fixed body and CTA, three hooks on top, so every set is a test.

05

Clear

Claims, approval codes and creator consent sorted before a single frame is shot.

06

Check

Footage reviewed against the brief: hook in the first seconds, claims said right.

07

Edit

Brand rules turned into editing guidelines, so the first cut is close to right.

08

Review

One feedback list per ad. Client status tracked apart from internal status.

09

Track

Delivered vs committed, every client, every month, with reasons for anything late.

10

Hand over

SOPs per team and recorded walkthroughs, so nothing depends on one person.
