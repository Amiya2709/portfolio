“When licensed toys showed up in our shots, the easy move was blaming creators. The real gap was ours.”

01

The brief

High monthly volume, every holiday landing on its date, every bear on brand.

02

The miss

Disney, Marvel and Pokémon products appeared in content. We had told creators what to feature, never what to avoid.

03

The fix

Owned it, then proposed four steps: a banned list from the client, sent to the store when a shoot is booked, given to creators, and added to our review check.
